Freezer restock — how it works

Five steps. Tap any one to see more.

The app signs you in with your work account — no button to press, it just goes.

On an iPad, do it in this order

  • Share → Add to Home Screen
  • Open it from the icon
  • Sign in there, on wifi
Order matters. The home-screen icon is its own app with its own memory. Sign in first and add the icon after, and you will just have to sign in again.
Wait for the green line: “Saved on this device.” That means it will open inside the freezer with no signal.

Then grab the laminated P- cards. The app tells you which number goes on each pallet.

This trips everyone up once, so it is worth thirty seconds.

CardGoes on whenWhat it means

The numbers count up all job and are never reused. P-7 means one pallet from the moment it hits the floor until it goes back up. The next job starts again at P-1.

The app walks you through one bay at a time, one pallet at a time.

Every line is one of these

TAKE OFF goes down to the floor for picking
SET ASIDE goes onto a different pallet — do not put it back on this one
STAYS nothing to do. It rides back up on the same pallet.

If a pallet went exactly as described, tap All as planned once and move on. Otherwise tap the line and say what really happened.

Cycle counts only ever appear on pallets that are already open. Nothing gets unwrapped just to be counted.

These are pushback lanes, so they pack forward. Load in the order the app shows and the right pallet ends up in front. Watch it:

A pallet usually does not go back where it came from. That is normal, not a mistake — the app works out the new spot and prints the placard for it. Put that placard on as it goes in.

Once the lane is loaded, the app asks two quick questions. Both take one tap.

  • “Does this look about right?” — we guess how full the pallet is. If it looks about right, tap Yep. If it is way off, tap No and pick the closest.
  • “Could another pallet fit in this lane?” — yes / one / two or more / not sure.
Finale cannot see either of those. You are the only source there is, and a rough answer beats none.

The plan is our best guess from the numbers in the computer. You are looking at the actual boxes. When those disagree, you are right — just tell the app.

None of this gets anyone in trouble. If you put 150 on the pallet we asked for and 50 somewhere else, tell the app both numbers. That is what keeps next week's plan right.

Other things worth knowing

  • Break button — the screen greys out so nothing gets tapped by accident.
  • Wrong tap? Tap the line again and change it. Nothing is locked.
  • It will not let you save — stop and tell someone. Do not keep working; nothing is being kept.

End of the shift

  • Sync — on wifi. This sends everything up.
  • Answer the last question: how many free pallet spaces are in the F1 back stock area.
  • Someone imports the stock changes into Finale.
  • Check back the next day that it all landed — the movement report only catches up overnight.
Use the app for the transfers this pull — not the Finale tablet. If both get used, the stock gets counted twice.
